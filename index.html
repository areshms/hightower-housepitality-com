Who we are

Hightower Housepitality LLC is an Ohio-based digital media and performance-marketing company. We build and operate online content properties and decision tools that help engaged adult audiences research and choose, with a focus on sports betting and iGaming media in US state-regulated markets.

What we do

  • Digital media properties. We develop informational websites, odds and comparison tools, and editorial content built for American sports fans of legal betting age.
  • Affiliate publishing. We partner with US state-licensed sportsbook and iGaming operators as an affiliate publisher, connecting our readers with their regulated platforms and products.
  • Performance marketing. We drive qualified, compliant traffic to our partners through search, content, and owned properties.

How we make money

Our revenue model is performance marketing: we earn affiliate commissions from licensed partners when our readers choose their products. As a publisher, our role ends at the referral — wagers are placed and accounts are held on the licensed platforms of our partners, never by us.

Our standards

  • Every partner is a state-licensed operator in a regulated US market.
  • Our content is created for audiences 21 and over, or the legal age in their jurisdiction.
  • Affiliate relationships are disclosed clearly on every applicable page.
